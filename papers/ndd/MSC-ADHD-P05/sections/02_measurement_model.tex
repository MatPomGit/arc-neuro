\section{EEG Measurement Model}

\subsection{Two distinct references}

The immediate preswitch task state and the target postswitch rule serve different measurement purposes.

The immediate preswitch interval is used to quantify \emph{challenge reactivity}. For band \(b\) and channel \(c\),

\[
R_{bc}^{(e)}
=
\log P_{bc,\mathrm{challenge}}^{(e)}
-
\log P_{bc,\mathrm{prechallenge}}^{(e)}.
\]

Recovery cannot use the same reference without qualification because Signal Gate changes the GO/NO-GO mapping. A stable calibration exposure to the target postswitch rule therefore supplies the recovery reference.

\subsection{Candidate spectral family}

The initial candidate bands are delta 1--4~Hz, theta 4--8~Hz, alpha 8--13~Hz, beta 13--30~Hz, and low gamma 30--45~Hz. These ranges are analysis conventions rather than one-to-one psychological states. Theta, alpha, and beta form the primary spectral family; delta and low gamma are secondary, with low gamma receiving stricter electromyographic-artifact scrutiny.

Theta/beta ratio is not a privileged primary endpoint and must not be interpreted as an ADHD diagnostic biomarker.

\subsection{Neural distance from the target-rule reference}

For quality-passed feature \(j=(b,c)\),

\[
z_j(t)
=
\frac{
\log P_j(t)
-
\operatorname{median}\!\left(\log P_{j,\mathrm{ref,target}}\right)
}{
1.4826\,\operatorname{MAD}\!\left(\log P_{j,\mathrm{ref,target}}\right)
+
\epsilon_j
}.
\]

Define the robust neural distance

\[
D_{\mathrm{EEG}}(t)
=
\operatorname{median}_{j\in Q(t)}
\left|z_j(t)\right|,
\]

where \(Q(t)\) contains only features passing the frozen signal-quality rule.

A candidate neural recovery latency is

\[
T_{\mathrm{EEG}}^{(e)}
=
\inf
\left\{
\tau \ge 0:
D_{\mathrm{EEG}}(t)\le\delta_{\mathrm{EEG}}
\text{ for }
w_{\mathrm{EEG}}
\text{ consecutive windows}
\right\}.
\]

Its time origin is the same \texttt{RECOVERY\_MONITORING} onset used by the core behavioral recovery endpoint. The EEG threshold, valid-feature requirement, stability count, and censoring rule are calibrated from measurement properties only. They cannot be selected to maximize association with behavioral \(T_{\mathrm{rec}}\), external symptoms, or treatment response.
