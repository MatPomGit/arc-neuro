\section{A Priori Taxonomy}

Before screening, recovery endpoints are provisionally classified along six orthogonal dimensions: (1) perturbation source, (2) reference-state definition, (3) recovery trajectory representation, (4) terminal recovery rule, (5) persistence or stability requirement, and (6) handling of censoring and missingness.

This taxonomy is deliberately provisional. New categories may be added if the literature contains conceptually distinct operationalizations, but additions will be documented without rewriting already-extracted records. The synthesis will distinguish reaction magnitude from recovery dynamics and will not treat a post-event difference score as a time-to-recovery measure unless an explicit temporal process is defined.
