Purpose: verify all recent records in `references/references.bib` against current publisher, PubMed/indexing, or official-organizational metadata before manuscript submission. This audit records the current bibliographic state; records that remain online-first or in press require another check immediately before submission.

## Verification rules

For journal articles, prefer the publisher version of record and use PubMed/indexing metadata as a cross-check where available. Verify:

- author list and author names;
- article title;
- journal title;
- publication year;
- volume and issue when assigned;
- page range or article number;
- DOI;
- online-first / in-press status.

For organizational guidance, verify the current official source and update date.

A DOI containing an earlier year is not treated as evidence that the bibliographic publication year is wrong; publication year follows the version-of-record metadata.
