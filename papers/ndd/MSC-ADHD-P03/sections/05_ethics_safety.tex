\section{Ethics and Safety}

The auditory experiment is an optional research substudy. Participation in the core intervention must not depend on accepting this component.

Sound level is bounded below device and hearing-safety limits established for the study. The child can stop the stimulus immediately without penalty. Headache, dizziness, discomfort, anxiety, sensory overload, or a request to stop terminates the experimental condition for that event and triggers the prespecified safety pathway.

The study should avoid therapeutic language suggesting that bilateral audio is a validated ADHD treatment. Consent materials must state that the specific effect of left-right alternation is uncertain and is precisely what the experiment is designed to test.

Because the manipulation uses stereo headphones, investigators should also monitor whether headphone use itself changes environmental awareness, comfort, or task performance. These effects belong to the safety and feasibility analysis even if they are unrelated to the hypothesized mechanism.
