## Figures and captions

1. **Observation and split diagram.** Latent generator → action/state → sampling/noise/missingness → observed sequence, with separate fit/validation/test blocks. The caption identifies what the analyst observes and what remains synthetic ground truth.
2. **Recovery maps.** Noise × observation interval, panels for generator and trajectory length. Use a common 0–1 probability scale, uncertainty companion panels and attempted counts. Missing cells have a distinct marker, never the color for zero recovery.
3. **Confusion matrices.** Rows: true synthetic family; columns: validation-selected candidate plus failed selection. Show counts and row percentages. Label full-set and nested-set matrices separately.
4. **Paired-loss forest plot.** Mean paired final-test difference and the frozen interval with ±epsilon band, panels by condition. Show loss units, number of paired replicates, and prospective equivalence rule. Do not derive epsilon from this plot.
5. **Parameter-recovery distributions.** True-value line, bias/dispersion and clipping/zero-gain panels. Mark fits where a nominal PID generator is functionally reduced by a zero integral/derivative gain.

## Interpretation and completion

Separate (a) family recovery, (b) predictive accuracy, (c) parameter precision and (d) observational equivalence. Better prediction does not establish a human mechanism. A broad interval is inconclusive, not evidence of equality. Failed estimation is a numerical/design outcome and cannot alone refute a psychological hypothesis.

Freeze loss, tolerance, interval method/level, comparisons, multiplicity, tie-breaking, degeneracy rules and grid before the confirmatory run. Preserve matched seed blocks for condition contrasts. Replicate counts follow desired Monte Carlo precision and compute feasibility, not favorable outcomes; for a proportion, the planning SE is sqrt[p(1−p)/R], with conservative p=0.5 when unknown. A finalized run manifest and checksum-backed output archive are required before replacing pending result text.
