The interval/equivalence summaries are pointwise developmental descriptions. The computational epsilon was 0.005, fixed before this run; it is not calibrated to a human instrument and is not the confirmatory tolerance. Correct family recovery need not imply a practically material prediction advantage.

Design: full two-level factorial. Limitations: fixed gains, a standardized feedback plant rather than the full ARC agent, known nonlinear width and switching threshold, conditional action prediction, MCAR gaps, and no parameter interval coverage. Sampling reconstructs histories from retained observations; AR/ARX lags become previous observed rather than previous latent actions after gaps.

The complete trajectory ledger is regenerated by the documented command instead of committed to Git. Its exact byte checksum and source/config hashes remain in metadata.json. Public results retain summary, confusion and parameter CSV files. Same-byte reproduction requires the recorded environment.
