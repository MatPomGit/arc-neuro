\section{Planned Results and Decision Outcomes}

All empirical results are pending. Reporting will separate content/cognitive evidence, development sample flow and exploratory structure from independent confirmatory structure. Reliability and retest estimates require uncertainty intervals; discriminant validity concerns overlap with adjacent constructs rather than a high reliability coefficient alone. Initial incremental criterion evidence must disclose coding quality, validation procedure and the hypothetical nature of the vignette criterion. Invariance claims require adequate subgroup information.

The reporting contract in \texttt{RESULTS\_PLAN.md} reserves five tables and three figures. If distinctness, structure or utility fails, report the failure and restrict, merge or abandon the candidate construct. Revisions informed by confirmation outcomes require new confirmation data. No controller-recovery or longitudinal dynamic results are included in this manuscript.
