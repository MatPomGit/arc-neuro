# M03: prospective psychometric protocol and measurement plan

Version 0.2, 2026-10-07. Proposed design, not a preregistration. Independent development and confirmation are mandatory; no ESM protocol is included.

## Research questions and proposed population

Study whether intentional attempts to change relational conditions form a distinct useful measure beyond agency/assertiveness, need for control, intolerance of uncertainty, attachment anxiety/avoidance and dominance/coercive control. Participants: consenting adults aged 18 or over, able to understand the final Polish materials and to refer to a current romantic relationship. A precise relationship-duration/context criterion must be frozen. No diagnosis-based exclusions or recruitment through one's own assessed students are proposed. Gender and relationship forms must not be restricted without scientific justification; invariance claims require adequate subgroup data.

## Phases and measurements

| Phase | Procedure | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Content | Independent expert ratings of relevance, clarity and overlap | Item-domain decisions; no construct confirmation |
| Cognitive pilot | Think-aloud/paraphrase interviews; notes by default | Comprehension, reference-period and anchor revision |
| Development | Candidate items, selected comparator measures, coarse demographics/context | EFA and item revision; cannot confirm its own structure |
| Confirmation | New participants, frozen items/scoring and comparisons | CFA, reliability, discriminant/incremental validity |
| Retest | Re-administer frozen scale after a justified fixed interval | Stability in a defined period; no daily monitoring |
| Brief criterion | Independently coded neutral vignette responses | Hypothetical intended behavior, not daily enacted behavior |

Do not move participants from development into independent confirmation. Keep members of the same couple in the same split if both happen to enter; record and account for dependence without collecting partner identities in the research payload. Default recruitment is individual and does not require a partner. No partner report or couple linkage is collected without a specific amendment. A retest subsample remains part of its source sample, not a new independent replication.

## Experimental item design

Reference proposal: current relationship and preceding four weeks, with explicit opportunity-to-act questions. Final period, response scale and scoring await cognitive testing. Distinguish frequency of attempts from success, partner consent, harm, relationship opportunity and general activity. Do not automatically sum all domains or invert distancing into insecurity.

Author-created examples for comprehension testing, not a validated scale or final item bank:

- „Proponowałam/proponowałem zmianę sposobu planowania wspólnego czasu.”
- „Inicjowałam/inicjowałem rozmowę o tym, kiedy i jak się kontaktujemy.”
- „Proponowałam/proponowałem nowe zasady dotyczące czasu dla siebie.”
- „Podejmowałam/podejmowałem próbę zmiany sposobu omawiania nieporozumień.”

Each example must be screened for overlap with assertiveness and communication competence. Failure to distinguish the construct is a valid result. Final complete wording, anchors, missing/not-applicable options and expected time must accompany the institutional application before recruitment. No norms or clinical cutoffs are proposed.

## Existing instruments

Candidate ECR-R is a contextual attachment comparator, not a controller label. Exact Polish adaptation, rights, authors/source, response format, scoring and missing-item rules remain to be verified before use. Other comparator instruments must be selected by construct coverage and burden; no unverified translation or protected item bank may be committed. Include evidence of their measurement quality in the actual sample. Optional M/F/S batteries are excluded from the default M03 design.

## Analysis freeze and sample-size justification

Before each phase specify item counts, estimator appropriate to response categories, EFA extraction/rotation and retention, CFA model/alternatives and numeric criteria, factor-correlation comparisons with adjacent constructs, reliability estimand and intervals, retest interval/estimand, missing-data rules, exclusions, multiplicity and stopping rules. A proposed independent criterion uses a rubric frozen before confirmation, blinded coders, coder agreement and opportunity/context covariates. Comparison against adjacent traits uses an untouched evaluation set or a prospectively specified validation procedure; do not call same-sample in-sample improvement generalization.

Plan sample sizes by simulated model/estimator behavior and precision of primary psychometric endpoints under plausible loadings, ordinal distributions, missingness and subgroup sizes. No universal participants-per-item rule or invented power result is adopted. Freeze numeric targets and ceilings separately for interviews, development, confirmation and retest. Confirmatory decisions cannot be revised after inspecting confirmation outcomes; failures are reported or motivate new data.

Burden: final item/time count per phase and pilot completion-time distributions must be recorded. Retest invitations are optional and private; no recurring daily notifications. No video, voice recording, location, messages, physiology or wearable data in the default protocol.
