\section{Ethics and Planned Reporting}

The human dossier is maintained in \texttt{human\_study/}. It requires its own institutional review, private individual consent, safe neutral notifications, skip/withdrawal controls, trained distress contact, and a controller-approved data-protection notice. Default procedures record no voice, video, messages, location or physiology. A partner is not entitled to individual responses. No personal controller, attachment or relationship diagnosis is returned. Approval, recruitment and data collection have not been established by this draft.

All results are pending. Planned outputs cover sample flow and overlap, schedule/burden and missingness, the primary incremental endpoint, separate within-person and later-time analyses, and any prospectively adopted secondary module. Five tables and three figures are specified in \texttt{RESULTS\_PLAN.md}. Report null predictive value, ambiguous dynamics and feasibility failure separately. Initial factor validation belongs to M03, and synthetic generator recovery belongs to M02.
