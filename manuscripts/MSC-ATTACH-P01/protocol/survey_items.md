# Draft ESM items for the H1 pilot

These items are conceptual English drafts. They are not yet a validated questionnaire and must be cognitively piloted. For a Polish-language study, translation/adaptation should be completed before preregistration.

## Response scale

Unless otherwise stated, use a continuous 0-100 visual analogue scale with labeled endpoints.

## Current relational state

### Perceived closeness, r_hat(t)

How close or connected do you feel to your partner right now?

0 = not close or connected at all  
100 = extremely close and connected

### Desired closeness, r*(t)

How close or connected would you ideally like to feel to your partner right now?

0 = not close or connected at all  
100 = extremely close and connected

The same anchors are used for perceived and desired closeness.

## Current affect/context

### Negative affect

How distressed, tense, upset, or emotionally unsettled do you feel right now?

0 = not at all  
100 = extremely

### Relational stressor

Since the previous prompt, was there a disagreement, rejection, disappointment, separation, or other event that noticeably affected how you felt about the relationship?

Response:
- no;
- yes.

If yes:

How strong was its impact on you?

0 = very small  
100 = extremely strong

## Regulatory action since previous prompt

### Approach/proximity seeking

Since the previous prompt, how much did you try to increase closeness, contact, reassurance, support, or emotional connection with your partner?

0 = not at all  
100 = very strongly

### Overt distancing/space seeking

Researcher interpretation: this item measures reported distancing behavior, not autonomy satisfaction or attachment avoidance. Participant-facing wording below is retained pending cognitive-pilot review.

Since the previous prompt, how much did you try to create more distance, space, independence, or reduced engagement with your partner?

0 = not at all  
100 = very strongly

### Internal suppression/disengagement

Since the previous prompt, how much did you try to suppress, minimize, ignore, or mentally distance yourself from relationship-related feelings without directly changing the interaction with your partner?

0 = not at all  
100 = very strongly

## Partner feedback

### Meaningful interaction

Since the previous prompt, did you have any meaningful interaction with your partner, in person or remotely?

- no;
- yes.

### Perceived responsiveness

If yes:

Since the previous prompt, how available, understanding, accepting, or responsive did your partner seem when you interacted?

0 = not at all responsive  
100 = extremely responsive

This item is a momentary operationalization and is not intended to replace a validated trait or relationship-level responsiveness scale.

## Contact context

Since the previous prompt, approximately how much meaningful contact did you have with your partner?

0 = none  
100 = almost continuous

Were you physically together for most of the interval?

- no;
- yes.

## Derived variables

Relational discrepancy:
e(t) = desired closeness - perceived closeness.

Signed relational action:
a_signed(t) = approach action - distancing action.

Positive e(t) indicates wanting more closeness than currently perceived. Negative e(t) indicates wanting less closeness than currently perceived.

The raw approach and distance items must always be retained, even when a_signed is used as the primary outcome.
